Silly Duel is a two-player party of tiny games. Play on one phone, or go online with a friend.

Win pies, earn coins, buy looks, power-ups and play rooms. First to the match score wins. Then someone pays the bill. Or not. We do not mind.

Made for friends, siblings and anyone who laughs when a pie hits a face.
